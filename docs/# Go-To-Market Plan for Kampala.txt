# Go-To-Market Plan for Kampala

## 1. Market Research & Positioning
- Identify top private and public schools in Kampala.
- Understand local administrative challenges and tailor messaging accordingly.

## 2. Product Localization
- Ensure the platform supports local languages and grading systems.
- Integrate with popular communication channels (SMS, WhatsApp).

## 3. Outreach & Awareness
- Partner with local education authorities and school associations.
- Organize workshops and free demo days for school administrators.
- Leverage social media and radio to reach school decision-makers.

## 4. Pilot Program
- Offer a 3-month free trial to select schools.
- Collect feedback and success stories for case studies.

## 5. Sales & Support
- Deploy a local sales team for in-person demos and onboarding.
- Provide dedicated support via phone, WhatsApp, and email.

## 6. Partnerships
- Collaborate with local ICT companies for integration and support.
- Explore partnerships with telecoms for bulk SMS rates.

## 7. Continuous Improvement
- Regularly update the platform based on user feedback.
- Host user forums and training sessions.
